# Worked examples

Each one is a real kind of case with invented figures. Trade rules give the source of each rule. Hebrew stays on its own line.

## 1. Water bill of a home client, in the spouse's name, read in the next month

The bill: net 400.00, VAT 72.00, total 472.00, dated in September. The open month is October. Bill for two earlier months, pages 3 and 4 are advertising.

- One row. Advertising pages are no documents.
- Bill in the spouse's name: normal for water (reg. 12c). No "other buyer" doubt on a home client.
- It is water, so 25% of expense and of VAT: row gross 118.00, net 100.00, VAT 18.00. Decide by what the document is.
- Dated before the open month: written in October and marked for review, because September is filed. Six months from its date are still open, so the VAT can be deducted.
- The expense account comes from the client's own chart.

## 2. Fuel of a car, client with a real chart

The invoice: net 200.00, VAT 36.00, total 236.00.

- VAT at two thirds, taken as 66% like Anna's Rivhit (reg. 18, Anna). 23.76 is deductible. The 12.24 that cannot be deducted stays in the expense.
- Row: gross 236.00, VAT 23.76, net 212.24.
- The app decides it by the account's name too (vehicle, fuel, parking, mobile). Rows saved before 7 Nov 2026 keep 66.67%: never change them silently.

## 3. Mobile phone bill

Net 100.00, VAT 18.00. VAT at 66%, as in example 2: 11.88. Internet at 100%. If one bill holds both and prints them apart, there are two rows. If it does not print them apart, one row, marked for review.

## 4. Invoice above the allocation ceiling

Invoice dated July 2026, net 5,000.01. No allocation number on it.

- The ceiling for July 2026 is 5,000 without VAT, by the invoice's date, so the VAT cannot be deducted without the number (s. 38(a1)).
- The row stays, marked, and a missing allocation number blocks the PCN file of a PCN client. The row is yellow and needs a person's tick.
- Net exactly 5,000.00 is not above the ceiling. A January invoice of net 8,000 is below that month's 10,000.
- With no VAT on it, no number is needed.

## 5. Invoice seven months old

An invoice dated 10 January, brought in during September. Eight months passed, so the deduction is late under s. 38(a).

- The row is written and marked, with the VAT flagged. The app's rule: more than six months late, "late"; exactly six, "check".
- The agent does not decide. Anna does.

## 6. Payment to the Tax Authority or National Insurance

A receipt for an income-tax advance or a VAT payment is not an expense. It stays as an inactive row, with its reason, so that nothing that came in is lost.
The same for a fine or a donation.

## 7. Credit note

Net -100.00, VAT -18.00. Net and VAT are negative in the row. It reduces input VAT and the expense. Do not "fix" the sign.

## 8. Mixed invoice

The VAT summary prints a liable group of net 1,000 with VAT 180, and an exempt group of 200. Two rows: 1,180 gross with VAT 180, and 200 gross with no VAT. Without printed groups, it is one row.

## 9. A photographed ledger of another program

Five photos of a printed list. Each line with its own date and amounts is a row. Headings, balances and totals are not. Its amounts already include any recognised share, so never apply 25% or two thirds to them again.

## 10. A document of a filed month

The open month is October, the document's month was filed and paid. It goes in the open month with a mark, unless it is one of Rivhit's own files, which go to their own month after a question to the user.

## 11. Client's own invoice in the pile

Supplier number equals the client's, usually with no buyer number. It is income, not an expense. Without VAT, as an exempt dealer issues, it has no VAT line.

## 12. Foreign currency

An invoice in dollars. The row goes to review and says to enter shekels at the rate of the date it was received. The app does not convert.

## 13. A VAT report that looks one shekel off

VAT payable is the difference of the rounded lines. Profit and loss is rounded once from exact sums. One shekel against another source is explained, not fixed. Say which line and why.
